# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | SQL + Power BI

## Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to identify trends in revenue, order volume, product performance, store performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The project combines extensive data cleaning and transformation in SQL with Power BI data visualization and dashboard development to turn raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on understanding what products are selling, where and when sales are occurring, and how sales performance changes over a 6 month period.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective was to use historical coffee shop transaction data to answer questions such as:

* How are total sales and order volume changing over time?
* Which store locations generate the most revenue?
* Which product categories and individual products perform best?
* How does sales performance differ between weekdays and weekends?
* What days and hours generate the highest sales?
* Are there identifiable trends that could help improve staffing, inventory planning, and sales strategy?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

A significant portion of the project involved preparing the raw transactional data for analysis using SQL.

The cleaning process included:

* Inspecting the raw dataset for data-quality issues
* Identifying and handling missing or inconsistent values
* Standardizing data formats and fields
* Creating and transforming date/time attributes
* Preparing weekday, weekend, month, and hour-level analysis fields
* Validating sales, order, and quantity calculations
* Structuring the data for efficient Power BI analysis

The goal was to ensure that the Power BI dashboard was built from a clean and analysis-ready dataset.

Please see the included SQL text file for a transparent look at the queries used.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The final Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of coffee shop performance across several dimensions.

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold
* Sales trends over time
* Month-over-month performance

For example, the dashboard reports approximately $157k in total sales, 33k orders, and 48k units sold across the month of May 2023.

## 📈 Sales Analysis

### Sales by Store Location

Sales were compared across the three store locations:

* Hell's Kitchen
* Lower Manhattan
* Astoria

The dashboard allows performance differences between locations to be quickly identified and compared.

### Sales by Product Category

Revenue was analyzed across product categories including:

* Coffee
* Tea
* Bakery
* Drinking Chocolate
* Coffee Beans
* Loose Tea
* Branded Products
* Flavors
* Packaged Chocolate

Coffee was the largest category in the dashboard, generating approximately **$60.4K in the displayed May period**, followed by Tea at approximately **$44.5K**.

### Top-Selling Products

The dashboard also identifies the highest-performing individual products.

Top performers in the displayed period included:

1. Barista Espresso
2. Brewed Chai Tea
3. Hot Chocolate
4. Gourmet Brewed Coffee
5. Brewed Black Tea
6. Brewed Herbal Tea
7. Premium Brewed Coffee
8. Organic Brewed Coffee
9. Scone
10. Drip Coffee

## 🕐 Time-Based Analysis

Sales were analyzed by:

* Day of week
* Hour of day
* Weekday vs. weekend
* Sales trend over time

The dashboard shows that approximately **74.4% of sales in the displayed May analysis occurred on weekdays**, compared with **25.6% on weekends**.

Hourly analysis was also used to identify periods of higher and lower sales activity, providing information that could potentially support staffing and operational decisions.
